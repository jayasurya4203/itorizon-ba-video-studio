Screening task · Business Analyst

Make a two-minute case for how you think.

This is a communication check, not a technical exam. Pick one topic, organise your view, practise to time, record a clean take, and review it against the brief before you submit.

Length2–3 minutes
FormatMP4 preferred
CameraOn, face visible
DeliveryNo script reading
What they are actually assessing
01

Clarity

Simple English, complete sentences, a pace the listener can follow without effort.

02

Structure

A beginning, two or three developed points, and a conclusion that lands somewhere.

03

Judgement

A real opinion supported by an example — not a list of definitions or slogans.

04

Presence

Eye contact, natural expression, appropriate setting and attire. No filters or effects.

Non-negotiables
  • 2–3 minute individual video, you visible on camera.
  • Speak to the camera, not to notes or a phone screen.
  • Your own thoughts, with at least one relevant example.
  • Minimal editing; no background music, filters, or effects.
  • Quiet, well-lit room; audible voice; professional attire.
  • Submit through the official candidate form.
Ten mistakes to avoid
  1. Running past three minutes.
  2. Reading a fully memorised script.
  3. Speaking about a topic you do not actually understand.
  4. Repeating the same point in different words.
  5. Giving an opinion with no supporting example.
  6. Speaking too fast or too softly.
  7. Looking away from the camera continuously.
  8. Adding music, filters, or unnecessary edits.
  9. Starting without introducing the topic.
  10. Ending abruptly without a takeaway.
Delivery tips that matter most

Voice

Use complete sentences at a conversational pace. Pause between ideas instead of filling space with “um”.

Camera

Lens at eye level, light in front of you, clean background. Look into the lens, not at your own image.

Thinking

Present a sequence, not a pile. Where possible, acknowledge the other side before giving your view.

Professionalism

Neat attire, quiet room, phone on silent. One take is better than ten highly edited ones.

Step 02 · Build the argument

Write the shape, not the script.

One or two lines per idea. The practice view turns these into cue cards you glance at — not a script you read. A natural pace is roughly 130 words a minute.
